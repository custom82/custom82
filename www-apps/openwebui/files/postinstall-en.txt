Open WebUI requires a Python ASGI server (uvicorn) to run.

Example (run as a dedicated user, behind a reverse proxy):
  cd @MY_HTDOCSDIR@/backend
  WEBUI_DATA_DIR=@MY_HOSTROOTDIR@/openwebui/data \
  uvicorn open_webui.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8080

Writable dirs (per vhost):
  @MY_HOSTROOTDIR@/openwebui/data
  @MY_HOSTROOTDIR@/openwebui/log
